Trade Safety

Last updated: 14 April 2026 · Read this before you trade anything.

Never do any of these

  • Never paste your Steam password or Steam Guard code on a website that isn't steamcommunity.com. SkinBox uses Steam OpenID. You will be redirected to the real Steam login — check the URL bar.
  • Never DM trade with another SkinBox user outside the platform. The entire value SkinBox provides is escrow and dispute resolution. Side deals bypass both and are the single biggest source of loss on peer-to-peer markets.
  • Never trust a "support agent" who DMs you on Discord, Steam, or Telegram. Our only support channel is support@skinbox.market and the in-app support form.
  • Never install a browser extension that claims to inflate your SkinBox listings, auto-accept trades, or "boost" your earnings. These are keyloggers.

How to verify a Steam trade offer is really for a SkinBox sale

  1. After a buyer commits on SkinBox, open the trade page (/profile/trades). The seller's and buyer's Steam profile URLs are printed there — those are the only two accounts that should appear on either end of the trade.
  2. Verify the items in the Steam offer exactly match the listing. If anything differs — even the rarity tier, or the name looks off by one character — decline the offer and open a dispute from the SkinBox trade page.
  3. Accept the offer using Steam Guard Mobile Authenticator. Never accept via email confirmation alone.
  4. After the offer clears in Steam, click "Confirm received" on the SkinBox trade page. Only that click releases the wallet funds to the seller.

What happens if a trade fails

  • The buyer's funds are held in the SkinBox wallet the instant they commit. Nothing moves to the seller until the buyer confirms receipt on the trade page — so if you never get an offer, nothing has been lost, and the escrow auto-cancels and refunds the buyer after 3 days of seller inactivity.
  • If the seller sent the trade but Steam put it on a 7-day trade hold, the funds stay locked in SkinBox until both sides confirm and the hold ages out.
  • If either party disputes the trade, staff pull the Steam trade-history evidence attached to the escrow and adjudicate within 48 hours. The wallet refunds or releases based on that decision.

Reporting a scam

Email abuse@skinbox.market with:

  • Your SkinBox username and the timestamp of the incident.
  • Screenshots of the scam (trade offer, DM, email, whatever).
  • The Steam profile URL of the scammer.

We triage within 24 hours and ban offending accounts from SkinBox. We also share scammer data with other marketplaces in the community.

What SkinBox can't do

We can't reverse a Steam trade after the 7-day hold. We can't compensate you for items you sent off-platform. We can't hunt down scammers in your jurisdiction. The best defence is prevention — and that starts with reading this page.
