Frequently asked questions

How long until I receive a sold item?expand_more
A successful trade clears in under a minute once both sides confirm on the Steam mobile app. Most buyers see the item in their inventory in 20–40 seconds.
When does the seller see funds?expand_more
Funds land in the seller wallet the moment Steam confirms the asset transfer. Withdrawals to Stripe-linked cards run on the next payout cycle.
What does SkinBox charge?expand_more
A flat 2% platform fee on the seller side. Buyers pay only the listed price; no surprise checkout add-ons.
Is my Steam account safe?expand_more
SkinBox uses Valve’s OpenID flow. We never see your password and never request your mobile authenticator. Trades go through your normal Steam offer screen.
Can I cancel a listing?expand_more
Yes — anytime before a buyer commits. After a Buy Now or accepted Bargain, the trade is locked and proceeds to Steam confirmation.
