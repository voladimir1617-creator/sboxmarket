New to SkinBox?
Work through the six-step walkthrough below, or jump straight to the FAQ. Still stuck? Open a support ticket at the bottom of this page.

routeGetting Started

1
login
Sign in with Steam
No password — OpenID handshake via steamcommunity.com.
2
account_balance_wallet
Top up your wallet
Stripe Checkout with a test card or real money. Funds arrive in seconds.
3
search
Browse the marketplace
Filter by category, rarity, price. Press / to jump straight into search.
4
shopping_cart
Buy Now or Place a Buy Order
Buy a listing instantly, or set a standing max price and walk away.
5

Receive the item
Listings you buy appear in your Platform Inventory on the Sell Items page, ready to relist.

Trade safely
Every write is signed, rate-limited, and audited. Support is one click away.

quizFrequently Asked Questions

SkinBox is a peer-to-peer marketplace for s&box cosmetic items. Sellers set their own price, and every listing is backed by a real Steam account. The platform is cheaper than going through the Steam store — a flat 2% on each sale vs Steam's 12%. Deposits and withdrawals are free.
